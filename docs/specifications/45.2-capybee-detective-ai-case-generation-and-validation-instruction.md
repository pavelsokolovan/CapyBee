# Spec 45.2 — CapyBee Detective AI Case Generation and Validation

**Depends on** [45.1](45.1-capybee-detective-catalog-and-case-setup-instruction.md) and the existing dynamic blueprint and presentation pipelines.

## Case generation

- Generate a fresh English or Polish case from the confirmed `classic-detective` settings. Keep the shared blueprint schema, bounded mechanics, disclosure, content review, and existing repair and call limits.
- Declare at least two suspects with `suspect-` actor IDs. Give the case two or three decisive `clue-` objectives. Each completes when a distinct development or challenge scene is visited. Write the observation as player-facing scene text and use spoiler-free objective copy.
- Label each accusation `accuse-<suspect-actor-id>`. The choice names that suspect and goes directly from resolution to an ending. Include one solved ending with a reconstruction that names the culprit, explains the method, motive and timeline, and quotes each decisive observation. Leave an alternative outcome for an inconclusive or incorrect result.

## Validation and presentation

- Use the engine's bounded state exploration to check every enabled accusation. The player must have visited all decisive clue scenes before the choice becomes available. Every route to the solved ending must take an accusation, and all such choices must identify the same suspect. Existing ending and objective reachability checks still apply.
- Require the solved reconstruction to include the named culprit and the exact decisive observations. The bilingual prompts direct how the evidence supports the deduction and distinguish red herrings. Structural checks establish access to evidence and consistency of references. Playtesting evaluates the quality of the reasoning.
- Preserve the accepted clue narration, accusation labels, and solved reconstruction through scene generation. Use the existing limited repair and blueprint-derived presentation fallback when provider text changes these fields. Keep validation findings free of story text.
- Apply the contract to the new detective profile version. Saved cases using earlier profiles keep their existing presentation behavior.

## Acceptance

- Exercise the production generation and validation path with an English and Polish case. Confirm that the success choice is gated, both endings are reachable, and invalid candidates use bounded repair.
- Verify that scene repair and provider-free presentation preserve the evidence, accusation labels, and reconstruction. Confirm an accepted case resumes with the same evidence.
